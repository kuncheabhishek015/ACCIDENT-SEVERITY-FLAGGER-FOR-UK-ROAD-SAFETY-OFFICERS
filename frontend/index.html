Road Safety Facts & Proven Council Interventions

Derived from 101,525 verified police collision records across Great Britain. Understand primary hazard drivers and how proactive highway engineering prevents fatal casualties.

💡 3.2x Severity Multiplier

1. Unlit Darkness & Streetlighting

  • The Hazard: Unlit rural darkness reduces driver sightline to under 18 meters, cutting reaction time by 65%.
  • Council Engineering Fix: Install high-output 4000K solar LED luminaires and solar reflective cat's eyes.
  • Driver Takeaway: Never overdrive your headlight beam; dip within 200m of oncoming vehicles.
Council Target: Priority illumination for unlit carriageway bends.
🌧️ 2.1x Skidding Rate

2. Wet Asphalt & Anti-Skid Grip

  • The Hazard: Rainwater creates a micro-film causing tire aquaplaning, halving friction from μ = 0.72 to μ = 0.35.
  • Council Engineering Fix: Apply High-Friction Surface Treatment (HFST) resin dressing + clearance of drainage gullies.
  • Driver Takeaway: Double following headway to at least 4 seconds; steer gently if wheels aquaplane.
Council Target: HFST resurfacing at approach zones to roundabouts and signals.
⚡ 4x Kinetic Energy

3. Kinetic Energy & Speed Curvature

  • The Hazard: Kinetic energy quadruples when speed doubles (E = ½mv²). 60 mph impacts are 2.4x more lethal than 30 mph.
  • Council Engineering Fix: Deploy Average Speed Cameras (SPECS) corridors and Vehicle-Activated Speed (VAS) signs.
  • Driver Takeaway: Speed limits are statutory ceilings, not safety targets in rain or darkness.
Council Target: Continuous speed moderation corridors on rural single carriageways.
🛑 62.4% Collision Share

4. Junctions & Staggered Turning Conflicts

  • The Hazard: T-Junctions and crossroads produce severe right-angle T-bone impacts due to restricted sightlines.
  • Council Engineering Fix: Convert priority junctions into compact mini-roundabouts and prune obscuring vegetation.
  • Driver Takeaway: Execute a double "Look Twice for Bikes" sweep before crossing oncoming traffic.
Council Target: Roundabout conversions at high-severity collision blackspots.
🌫️ Multi-Car Pileups

5. Fog Obscuration & Spatial Disorientation

  • The Hazard: Dense fog creates visual deception of distance, leading drivers to tailgate at high speeds until impact.
  • Council Engineering Fix: Variable Message Signs (VMS) with automated radar speed warnings during fog events.
  • Driver Takeaway: Use dedicated fog lights; disengage cruise control immediately in mist or fog.
Council Target: Automated VMS advisory gantries on trunk corridors.
🚶 85% Survival at 20 mph

6. Vulnerable Road Users & 20 mph Zones

  • The Hazard: Pedestrian survival drops from 85% at 20 mph to below 15% at 40 mph vehicle impact speed.
  • Council Engineering Fix: Establish 20 mph school safety zones, raised pedestrian tables, and high-visibility zebra crossings.
  • Driver Takeaway: Scan footpaths and anticipate pedestrian crossings around bus stops and school gates.
Council Target: 20 mph school perimeter calming and raised crossings.
